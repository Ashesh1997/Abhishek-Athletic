SCENE 1 // UPGRADE DETAILS ABHISHEK'S ATHLETIC

Program Upgrades

These modular modifications have been carefully structured into the baseline program to transform it from a general bodybuilding structure into a world-class athletic preparation blueprint.

01. Loaded Carries (Twice Weekly)

Incorporated as high-load structural finishes. Single-arm Suitcase walks on Thursdays target unilateral quadratus lumborum bracing, and Heavy bilateral Farmer Walks on Saturdays target grip strength and trap hypertrophy.

02. Rotational Core Integration

Sprinting mechanics require transverse-plane power. Dynamic medicine ball wall throws (Thursdays) and anti-rotational Pallof presses (Thursdays/Saturdays) lock in oblique force-transfer capabilities.

03. Active Zone 2 Decompression

Wednesdays now host dedicated low-threshold Zone 2 aerobic blocks. Designed strictly around active nasal oxygenation to promote circulatory capillary pathways, speed up metabolic clearance, and downregulate CNS parameters.

04. Neck & Grip Shields

Heavy pinch holds and barbell hanging frames integrated on Tuesday/Friday upper blocks protect wrist mechanics, while targeted neck flexion/extensions build posterior head support.
