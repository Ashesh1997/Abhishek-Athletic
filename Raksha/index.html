Success Rules

At 37.9% body fat, consistency is your ultimate physiological multiplier. Apply these core principles to ensure complete metabolic adaptation during Month 1.

01. Never Miss Two in a Row

Consistency over perfection. If a session is interrupted, ensure the following scheduled workout is executed without fail to protect the training habit.

02. Cumulative Daily Walking

Walk every single day. Non-exercise thermogenesis (NEAT) controls the majority of your daily calorie expenditure. Track weekly steps: 6k to 10k goal progression.

03. High-Quality Sleep (7-8 hours)

Fat loss and muscle repair occur during deep delta sleep phases. Hormonal balance, specifically ghrelin and cortisol control, requires a strict 7-8 hour schedule.

04. Adequate Hydration (2.5-3 Liters)

Drinking 2.5–3 liters of water daily helps flush byproducts of fat burning, maintains muscle hydration, and controls hunger levels during your caloric deficit.
